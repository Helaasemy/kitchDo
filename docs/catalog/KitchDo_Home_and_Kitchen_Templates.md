# KitchDo template catalog for Home and Kitchen

Product concept and researched starter content for the existing mobile app  
9 November 2026

**How the catalog works**

Someone should be able to type what they need to do and see a useful checklist immediately. A person searching “clean the bathroom” should find a short bathroom routine. Someone typing “open a café” should find the opening-shift checklist.

| Element | Proposed behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Library | One catalog with two main categories, Home and Kitchen. Home covers household routines. Kitchen covers cafés, restaurants, and food trucks, with checklists grouped by the work they support. Use the active workspace to prioritize results and keep an All results view available. |
| Search | Match titles, alternate phrases, and task topics. Use everyday terms such as “Sunday reset,” “meal prep,” “cash up,” “paperwork,” and “fridge check.” Let users distinguish one-time projects, recurring routines, and as-needed work. |
| Preview | Show the checklist sections, a few example tasks, and suggested timing. Keep equipment, setup, and location differences visible before users copy a list. A one-time launch stays in progress across days, with one set of checkmarks per launch. |
| Use | Copy the preset into the workspace as an editable checklist. Users can remove irrelevant steps, start immediately, or choose a repeat schedule. Keep Create from scratch available alongside the catalog. |
| Timing | Give each template a suggested cadence. Use the current app's available scheduling options. The Part 1 brief describes daily and selected-weekday schedules. If monthly or seasonal scheduling is still absent in the shipped app, those presets can be used manually until the relevant repeat option exists. |
| Team use | When the workspace supports invitations and assignments, assign a real occurrence to one employee first. For example, Maya owns today's café opening and Sam owns today's deep clean. Item-level assignments can follow when splitting one checklist between stations becomes a regular need. |
| Updates | Keep the catalog master, the user's editable copy, and each completed occurrence separate. A catalog update can improve new copies while preserving the user's edits and existing history. |

**Suggested featured templates**  
**Home** — Weekly home reset, Clean the bathroom, Plan meals for the week, Prep meals for the week, Monthly home deep clean, and Monthly household paperwork.  
**Kitchen** — Open the café for service, Close the café after service, Clean the café daily, Clean the espresso station, Receive a café delivery, and Weekly café paperwork.

The checklists below use original wording. The reference notes explain where the research informed the checklist design. Suggested frequencies are editable defaults. For food businesses, adapt each checklist to the site's own procedures, equipment instructions, and applicable local guidance. Location-specific startup requirements belong in clearly labeled local notes or editions.

## Home

### H01 · Evening kitchen reset

*Daily · Search terms — dishes, tidy kitchen, closing shift at home*

- [ ] Put away food and deal with leftovers.
- [ ] Load the dishwasher or wash the dishes.
- [ ] Clear and wipe the counter and dining table.
- [ ] Wipe spills from the hob after it has cooled.
- [ ] Rinse and wipe the sink.
- [ ] Take out rubbish or recycling if the bins are full.
- [ ] Set out anything needed for breakfast.

### H02 · Weekly home reset

*Weekly · Search terms — weekly cleaning, Sunday reset, apartment chores*

- [ ] Put loose items back in their rooms.
- [ ] Dust the main surfaces.
- [ ] Wipe kitchen and bathroom surfaces.
- [ ] Replace used towels and kitchen cloths.
- [ ] Vacuum or sweep the main rooms.
- [ ] Mop suitable floors where needed.
- [ ] Empty bins and sort recycling.
- [ ] Pick one extra area to tackle this week.

### H03 · Clean the bathroom

*Weekly · Search terms — toilet, shower, bathroom cleaning*

- [ ] Gather the bathroom cleaning supplies.
- [ ] Wash the sink and wipe around the taps.
- [ ] Clean the mirror.
- [ ] Clean the toilet bowl, seat, and outside surfaces.
- [ ] Wash the bath or shower surfaces.
- [ ] Empty the bin and replace used towels.
- [ ] Sweep and mop the floor.

### H04 · Laundry day

*Weekly or as needed · Search terms — clothes, washing machine, wash and fold*

- [ ] Gather laundry and empty pockets.
- [ ] Sort loads using the garment care labels.
- [ ] Treat stains where appropriate.
- [ ] Wash each load using a suitable setting.
- [ ] Hang garments or dry them according to the labels.
- [ ] Clean the dryer's lint screen if you used a dryer.
- [ ] Fold and put away the clean laundry.

### H05 · Change bedding and towels

*Weekly or as needed · Search terms — sheets, linen, bedroom reset*

- [ ] Strip the beds that need fresh linen.
- [ ] Collect used towels and bath mats.
- [ ] Start suitable wash loads.
- [ ] Make the beds with fresh sheets and pillowcases.
- [ ] Put out clean towels.
- [ ] Dry and store the washed linen.

### H06 · Monthly home deep clean

*Monthly · Search terms — monthly cleaning, deep clean, baseboards*

- [ ] Wipe skirting boards and reachable door trim.
- [ ] Dust reachable ledges and tops of furniture.
- [ ] Wipe marks from washable doors and cabinet fronts.
- [ ] Vacuum under movable furniture where it is easy to reach.
- [ ] Clean the inside of the most-used bin.
- [ ] Wipe reachable window sills and tracks.
- [ ] Wash or vacuum removable mats according to their care instructions.
- [ ] Choose one appliance or cupboard for a separate cleaning session.

### H07 · Reset the fridge and pantry

*Weekly · Search terms — fridge clean-out, use up food, cupboards*

- [ ] Check chilled and pantry food before shopping.
- [ ] Put food you plan to use soon in an easy-to-see spot.
- [ ] Check labels and storage dates and discard food that should go.
- [ ] Wipe visible spills and crumbs.
- [ ] Move older suitable stock in front of newer purchases.
- [ ] Put one use-it-up meal on the plan.
- [ ] Add low staples to the shopping list.

### H08 · Clean the dishwasher

*Monthly or per manual · Search terms — dishwasher filter, appliance care*

- [ ] Find the maintenance instructions for your model.
- [ ] Empty the dishwasher.
- [ ] Clean removable parts the manual identifies.
- [ ] Check the spray arms for visible blockages as directed.
- [ ] Wipe the door edges and seals as directed.
- [ ] Run the recommended cleaning cycle if due.
- [ ] Note leaks or persistent problems for service.

### H09 · Care for the washer and dryer

*Monthly or per manual · Search terms — washing machine maintenance, dryer care*

- [ ] Check the care instructions for each appliance.
- [ ] Wipe the washer door seal and remove visible debris as directed.
- [ ] Clean the detergent drawer if the model allows removal.
- [ ] Run the recommended drum-cleaning cycle if due.
- [ ] Check the dryer lint screen and accessible areas listed in the manual.
- [ ] Record any leak, unusual noise, or drying problem for follow-up.

### H10 · Monthly home maintenance walk-through

*Monthly · Search terms — apartment maintenance, home repairs, renter checklist*

- [ ] Look for new leaks or damp spots around sinks and toilets.
- [ ] Check doors, windows, and handles for problems.
- [ ] Check for unusually slow drains.
- [ ] Check whether any manufacturer-recommended filter service is due.
- [ ] List repairs and note when each problem first appeared.
- [ ] Report issues that belong to your landlord or building manager.
- [ ] Book help for repairs you are responsible for.

### H11 · Test smoke alarms

*Monthly or per local guidance · Search terms — smoke detector, fire safety reminder*

- [ ] Locate each smoke alarm in the home.
- [ ] Test each alarm using its test button and the model's instructions.
- [ ] Arrange prompt battery replacement, replacement of the alarm, or landlord follow-up for a failed test as appropriate.
- [ ] Make sure exits and their routes are clear.
- [ ] Record the test date and any unresolved issue.

### H12 · Plan meals for the week

*Weekly · Search terms — meal plan, dinners, weekly menu*

- [ ] Check what is already in the fridge, freezer, and cupboards.
- [ ] Mark evenings when nobody needs a cooked dinner.
- [ ] Choose meals that use ingredients already on hand.
- [ ] Pick one quick backup meal.
- [ ] Plan when to eat leftovers or freeze extra portions.
- [ ] Make a list of ingredients you still need.
- [ ] Pick a day to shop and a day to prep.

### H13 · Get ready for the grocery shop

*Weekly or as needed · Search terms — groceries, supermarket, shopping list*

- [ ] Check the meal plan for missing ingredients.
- [ ] Check breakfast, snack, and household staples.
- [ ] Add the quantities you need.
- [ ] Group items by aisle or food category.
- [ ] Check whether bags or a cooler are needed.
- [ ] Check the list before checkout and put groceries away when home.

### H14 · Prep meals for the week

*Weekly · Search terms — Sunday meal prep, packed lunches, meal boxes*

- [ ] Pick the meals, portions, and days to prep for.
- [ ] Gather recipes, ingredients, and suitable containers.
- [ ] Wash produce as appropriate and prepare recipe ingredients.
- [ ] Cook the components you plan to make ahead.
- [ ] Divide cooked food into meal portions and store it promptly using local food safety guidance.
- [ ] Label containers with contents and prep date.
- [ ] Freeze portions you plan to eat later if suitable.
- [ ] Clean work surfaces and put equipment away.

### H15 · Batch-cook and freeze portions

*As needed · Search terms — freezer meals, batch cooking, make ahead*

- [ ] Pick a freezer-friendly recipe and check available space.
- [ ] Decide how many portions to make.
- [ ] Gather containers and labels before cooking.
- [ ] Cook the batch using the chosen recipe.
- [ ] Portion, cool, and store it using local food safety guidance.
- [ ] Label with dish name and date.
- [ ] Add the portions to your freezer list.
- [ ] Put a freezer meal on a future meal plan.

### H16 · Cook a pot of red lentil soup

*As needed · Search terms — cook lentil soup, vegetarian dinner, soup meal prep*

- [ ] Choose the recipe and scale it to the portions you need.
- [ ] Gather red lentils, vegetables, and the broth and seasonings the recipe calls for.
- [ ] Prepare the vegetables and lentils as directed.
- [ ] Start the vegetable base and add broth and lentils following the recipe.
- [ ] Simmer until the lentils are tender and finish the seasoning.
- [ ] Set aside portions to cool and store safely if making extra.
- [ ] Wash the pot and reset the kitchen.

An example recipe with ingredient quantities and cooking steps is available from the NHS. [Vegetable and lentil soup](https://www.nhs.uk/healthier-families/recipes/vegetable-and-lentil-soup/)

### H17 · Cook a tray of chickpeas and vegetables

*As needed · Search terms — tray bake, sheet-pan dinner, vegetarian dinner*

- [ ] Choose a recipe and check oven temperature and timing.
- [ ] Gather the vegetables, chickpeas, seasoning, and tray.
- [ ] Prepare the vegetables and chickpeas as directed.
- [ ] Season and spread the ingredients on the tray.
- [ ] Roast according to the recipe and check when the vegetables are tender.
- [ ] Add a side or topping if planned.
- [ ] Store any leftovers promptly using local food safety guidance.

An example recipe with ingredient quantities and oven timing is available from Good Food. [Roast summer vegetables and chickpeas](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/roast-summer-vegetables-chickpeas)

### H18 · Monthly household paperwork

*Monthly · Search terms — bills, renewals, home admin, documents*

- [ ] Gather bills, letters, and receipts that need action.
- [ ] Check what is due before next month.
- [ ] Review upcoming renewals and appointments.
- [ ] File receipts or documents you need to keep.
- [ ] Set reminders for unresolved items and record their due dates.
- [ ] Follow up on one pending household request.

### H19 · Check pet supplies

*Weekly or as needed · Search terms — dog supplies, cat supplies, pet chores*

- [ ] Check the remaining pet food and litter or waste bags.
- [ ] Wash bowls as appropriate and check where food is stored.
- [ ] Check bedding and clean it if needed.
- [ ] Inspect leads, harnesses, carriers, or frequently used toys for wear.
- [ ] Confirm the next appointment or refill date already set by your vet.
- [ ] Add supplies you are low on to the shopping list.

### H20 · Prepare for a move

*One-time · Search terms — moving house, moving apartment, packing checklist*

- [ ] Confirm moving and key handover dates.
- [ ] Book transport, movers, or helpers.
- [ ] Decide what to keep, donate, recycle, or sell.
- [ ] Label boxes by destination room.
- [ ] Pack a first-night bag and keep essential documents accessible.
- [ ] Arrange address and utility updates that apply to you.
- [ ] Check the old place room by room before handing over keys.
- [ ] Record any condition or meter readings your handover requires.

### H21 · Get the house ready for guests

*As needed · Search terms — visitors, hosting, guest room*

- [ ] Confirm arrival time and guest count.
- [ ] Ask about meal needs if you are serving food.
- [ ] Clear the entryway and main seating area.
- [ ] Put out fresh hand towels and restock the bathroom.
- [ ] Make the guest bed and set out spare towels if needed.
- [ ] Prepare drinks, snacks, or meal ingredients.
- [ ] Share arrival or access instructions.

### H22 · Check houseplants

*Weekly check · Search terms — watering plants, plant care*

- [ ] Check each plant using its species-specific care guidance.
- [ ] Water plants that actually need it.
- [ ] Empty standing water from saucers where appropriate.
- [ ] Look for visible pests, damage, or yellowing.
- [ ] Remove dead leaves where appropriate.
- [ ] Record plants that may need a new pot or a different spot.

### Opening and closing shifts

#### B02 · Open the café for service

*Daily before opening · Search terms — opening shift, café opener, barista opening*

- [ ] Review the previous shift's handover and outstanding issues.
- [ ] Start opening equipment using the site's routine.
- [ ] Complete refrigeration and display checks in the site's log.
- [ ] Check handwashing stations are stocked.
- [ ] Confirm that counters, tools, and customer areas are ready.
- [ ] Set up the espresso station and check the first shot.
- [ ] Restock cups, milk, beans, and other service supplies.
- [ ] Confirm what's available and check allergen information for menu changes.
- [ ] Start the till and confirm the payment terminal is ready.
- [ ] Brief the team on expected orders, shortages, and issues.
- [ ] Unlock the entrance at opening time.

#### B03 · Close the café after service

*Daily after service · Search terms — closing shift, café closer, end of day*

- [ ] Finish serving and check that open orders are handled.
- [ ] Label and store remaining food under the site's procedure.
- [ ] Record items discarded according to the site's routine.
- [ ] Clean the espresso station and service counters.
- [ ] Finish dishwashing and put away clean equipment.
- [ ] Empty bins and clean the floors.
- [ ] Complete the till and payment close where assigned.
- [ ] Leave a note about shortages, maintenance, and unfinished work.
- [ ] Put equipment into the correct overnight state.
- [ ] Check the premises and secure doors and alarms.

#### B16 · Open a restaurant kitchen line

*Before each service · Search terms — restaurant opening, kitchen opener, line check*

- [ ] Review bookings, specials, and expected covers.
- [ ] Confirm station coverage and prep priorities.
- [ ] Start station equipment using the site's procedures.
- [ ] Complete required equipment and food-storage checks.
- [ ] Check the quality, quantity, and labels of prepared items.
- [ ] Refill station tools, plates, and service supplies.
- [ ] Confirm menu changes and share allergen updates with service staff.
- [ ] Brief the line on shortages and dishes unavailable for this service.

#### B17 · Close a restaurant kitchen line

*After each service · Search terms — restaurant closing, line breakdown, kitchen closer*

- [ ] Confirm the final orders are complete.
- [ ] Record and store eligible remaining food using the site's procedures.
- [ ] Record disposals, shortages, and prep needed next shift.
- [ ] Break down and clean each station using its cleaning plan.
- [ ] Finish dishwashing and return station tools.
- [ ] Complete the closing food safety records.
- [ ] Leave equipment in the approved overnight state.
- [ ] Hand over unresolved issues to the next lead.

#### B18 · Set up a food truck for service

*Before each service · Search terms — mobile catering, food truck opening, market stall*

- [ ] Confirm the site, service hours, and arrival arrangements.
- [ ] Position and secure the vehicle according to the operator's procedure.
- [ ] Complete water, power, and equipment checks required for the unit.
- [ ] Set up handwashing and waste stations.
- [ ] Complete required food storage and service checks.
- [ ] Check menu availability and allergen information.
- [ ] Test the order and payment setup.
- [ ] Prepare the service window and queue area.

### Cleaning and equipment maintenance

#### B04 · Clean the café daily

*Daily · Search terms — cleaning checklist, front of house, café cleaning*

- [ ] Clean service and food-contact surfaces using the site's method.
- [ ] Wash service utensils and trays.
- [ ] Wipe spills in the display and counter areas.
- [ ] Clean customer tables, chair surfaces, and high-touch points.
- [ ] Clean staff and customer washrooms using their assigned supplies.
- [ ] Refill handwashing and cleaning supplies.
- [ ] Empty bins and replace liners.
- [ ] Clean floors and return cleaning equipment to storage.
- [ ] Flag areas that could not be cleaned as planned.

#### B05 · Deep-clean the café

*Weekly as a starting point · Search terms — weekly cleaning, deep cleaning rota, cleaning schedule*

- [ ] Pick the areas due under the site's cleaning plan.
- [ ] Empty and clean accessible shelving sections in stages.
- [ ] Clean under and behind movable items where safe and permitted.
- [ ] Clean the hard-to-reach areas of the service counter and display.
- [ ] Clean bin storage and surrounding surfaces.
- [ ] Check stock of approved cleaning supplies.
- [ ] Record damaged surfaces, signs of pests, or repairs to escalate.
- [ ] Confirm the next cleaning date for work that could not be completed.

#### B06 · Clean the espresso station

*Daily or per manual · Search terms — espresso machine cleaning, coffee bar cleanup, grinder*

- [ ] Confirm the routine and supplies for this espresso machine and grinder.
- [ ] Clean baskets and portafilters as directed.
- [ ] Clean group heads and backflush only if required by the machine's instructions.
- [ ] Clean steam wands, drip trays, and the surrounding work area.
- [ ] Clean milk jugs and other service tools.
- [ ] Clean the grinder parts covered by the routine for this model.
- [ ] Report leaks, weak steam, or other faults.

#### B19 · Review café equipment maintenance

*Monthly or per service plan · Search terms — espresso machine service, maintenance log, equipment repair*

- [ ] Check upcoming service dates against equipment manuals.
- [ ] Review faults reported since the last check.
- [ ] Confirm which equipment requires a qualified service visit.
- [ ] Book work and plan how the affected station will operate.
- [ ] Update the service log after completed work.
- [ ] Tell the team about equipment unavailable for use.

### Food preparation

#### B07 · Prep food before café service

*Daily or per shift · Search terms — kitchen prep, pastry display, sandwiches, prep list*

- [ ] Review the expected demand and menu for the shift.
- [ ] Check usable stock before starting prep.
- [ ] Pick what to make and set target quantities.
- [ ] Prepare the station using the site's separation and hygiene procedures.
- [ ] Follow current recipe and ingredient specifications.
- [ ] Label and store prepared items under the site's procedure.
- [ ] Refill the display or service station as planned.
- [ ] Report shortages and update availability before service.

### Stock and deliveries

#### B08 · Receive a café delivery

*Each delivery · Search terms — goods in, stock delivery, supplier order*

- [ ] Match the supplier and items against the order.
- [ ] Count the delivered items and record discrepancies.
- [ ] Inspect packaging condition and date markings.
- [ ] Complete any required temperature checks using the site's procedure.
- [ ] Flag substitutions before ingredients are used.
- [ ] Handle and document rejected items under the site's procedure.
- [ ] Put accepted stock into appropriate storage promptly.
- [ ] File the delivery record and follow up on outstanding items.

#### B09 · Count stock and plan reorders

*Weekly or before an order · Search terms — inventory count, stock take, order supplies*

- [ ] Count stock in the bar, kitchen, and storage areas.
- [ ] Include packaging, cleaning supplies, and other consumables.
- [ ] Note stock approaching its use date and any damaged items.
- [ ] Check booked events, expected demand, and incoming orders.
- [ ] Compare counts against your target stock levels.
- [ ] Make the reorder list and confirm order cutoffs.
- [ ] Record shortages the next shift needs to know about.

### Food safety and inspections

#### B13 · Review a changed ingredient or menu item

*Whenever an ingredient, supplier, or recipe changes · Search terms — allergens, substitutions, menu update*

- [ ] Identify the ingredient or preparation that changed.
- [ ] Obtain the current label or specification from the supplier.
- [ ] Check which menu items and service channels are affected.
- [ ] Review the change under the site's allergen and cross-contact procedures.
- [ ] Update the relevant allergen record and customer-facing information.
- [ ] Brief staff before the changed item is served.
- [ ] Hold the item and ask the responsible lead when information remains unconfirmed.

#### B14 · Review the café's food safety records

*Weekly or monthly per site procedure · Search terms — food safety log, hygiene records, documentation*

- [ ] Check that required records can be found and are current.
- [ ] Review recent opening, closing, and cleaning records.
- [ ] Review missed checks and actions taken.
- [ ] Check whether menu or supplier changes require record updates.
- [ ] Review unresolved maintenance, pest, or hygiene issues with the lead.
- [ ] Assign follow-up and record review dates.
- [ ] Update staff guidance when a site procedure changes.

#### B20 · Prepare for a food safety visit

*As needed · Search terms — inspection checklist, audit preparation, restaurant documentation*

- [ ] Review the relevant local authority's guidance for the site.
- [ ] Gather current food safety procedures and the records they reference.
- [ ] Check that cleaning, supplier, and training records are accessible.
- [ ] Check the current allergen information against the actual menu.
- [ ] Walk through the premises and record problems to resolve.
- [ ] Confirm who will meet the visitor and where records are stored.
- [ ] Record follow-up actions after the visit.

### Team training and handovers

#### B10 · Hand over the café shift

*Each handover · Search terms — shift handoff, pass to next team, manager notes*

- [ ] List open orders or customer issues.
- [ ] Share sold-out items, substitutions, or pending deliveries.
- [ ] Flag equipment faults and safety concerns already raised.
- [ ] Name the unfinished tasks and who should follow up.
- [ ] Highlight time-sensitive work for the incoming shift.
- [ ] Confirm the incoming lead has received the handover.

#### B15 · Onboard a new café employee

*One-time per hire · Search terms — staff training, first shift, new barista*

- [ ] Introduce the shift lead and show the layout of the site.
- [ ] Set up approved access to the systems the role uses.
- [ ] Explain the employee's station and first-shift expectations.
- [ ] Show how to find hygiene, allergen, and incident procedures.
- [ ] Demonstrate the opening or closing tasks the employee will perform.
- [ ] Pair the employee with a trainer for supervised practice.
- [ ] Record the training covered and any follow-up still needed.

### Cash and paperwork

#### B11 · Close and reconcile the till

*Daily or per cash shift · Search terms — cash up, cash drawer, POS close*

- [ ] Check for unresolved sales or open orders in the POS.
- [ ] Run the required shift or day-end report.
- [ ] Count the cash drawer following the business's procedure.
- [ ] Compare cash and other payments with the POS totals.
- [ ] Record discrepancies and escalate according to policy.
- [ ] Prepare deposits and handle floats under the site's procedure.
- [ ] File or hand over the close report.

#### B12 · Weekly café paperwork

*Weekly · Search terms — admin, paperwork, owner checklist, invoices*

- [ ] Collect supplier invoices and receipts.
- [ ] Match deliveries with invoices and query differences.
- [ ] Review payments, renewals, and bookings coming due.
- [ ] Review timesheet exceptions if the café has staff.
- [ ] File current business records in the agreed location.
- [ ] Check pending authority, landlord, or contractor correspondence.
- [ ] Give each unresolved admin task an owner and follow-up date.
